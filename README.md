# TV Tracking

A curated catalogue of English-language television originating in North America or Britain, beginning in 1950. Streaming originals are included.

## Selection criteria

- **Worth watching today:** entertainment, storytelling, and performances.
- **Strong reputation:** audience response, critical recognition, and lasting appeal.
- **A clear reason to recommend it:** each pick offers something worthwhile beyond historical importance.

Each decade has a shared ceiling of 50 recommendations across all eligible formats. A list can be shorter. Ratings and awards support the editorial judgement. User decisions determine which recommendations remain in the active catalogue; approved titles appear in their original recommendation order.

Full recommendation batches are retained in [RAW](RAW/README.md) with their original numbering and links for other people to review independently. The [complete 1960s batch contains all 50 shows](RAW/1960s.md).

## Scope

Include scripted series, scripted comedy and drama anthologies, reality series and competitions, miniseries, and docuseries. Major game shows must have more than five seasons for that particular version and meet the selection criteria. Feature-length episodes within an eligible series are allowed.

Exclude animation and cartoons; standalone movies, TV movies, and side movies; news and current affairs; local-only programmes; sports coverage; and talk shows. Pure variety, music, instructional, and classroom programmes fall outside the included formats.

Keep one entry per distinct series or edition. Seasons, individual episodes, broadcasters, and streaming services do not create additional entries. Remakes, remasters, and separately identifiable re-release editions are distinct entries under the owner's rules and must meet the same selection criteria. Ordinary repeated airings do not create additional entries.

The original television premiere determines the decade, including when a programme previously existed on radio. Series with the same name can have separate entries when they are distinct versions.

## Research

Title identities and premiere dates are checked against series records and source references in the data. The selection also draws on the [Television Academy's programme histories](https://interviews.televisionacademy.com/shows/twilight-zone-the), [BFI criticism](https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/where-begin-with-nigel-kneale), [BFI's BBC programme survey](https://www.bfi.org.uk/lists/100-bbc-tv-gamechangers), and [National Film Board curation](https://blog.nfb.ca/blog/2018/04/24/candid-eye-series/).
